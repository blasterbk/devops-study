## Real-Life Analogy

Think of Kueue Cohorts and GPU Borrowing as **a cooperative corporate equipment lending program between two engineering departments in the same company**:

- **The Rigid Partition Waste (Without Cohorts):**
  - Team NLP has a budget for 8 GPUs (**`nominalQuota: 8`**).
  - Team Vision has a budget for 8 GPUs (**`nominalQuota: 8`**).
  - On Friday night, Team Vision goes home for the weekend (**8 GPUs sit 100% idle and wasted, costing \$5,000 in unused cloud fees**).
  - Team NLP has a critical LLM training deadline, but their queue is blocked because they only have 8 GPUs (**Artificial Bottleneck**).
- **The Cooperative Cohort Treaty (`cohort: research-rnd`):**
  - Team NLP and Team Vision sign a sharing agreement (**Join the same Cohort**).
  - The rule: *"If Team Vision is asleep, Team NLP is allowed to borrow their 8 idle GPUs (**`borrowingLimit: 8`**) and train on all 16 GPUs at double speed!"*
- **The Automatic Reclamation Rule (Preemption & Eviction):**
  - On Monday at 9:00 AM, Team Vision comes into work and submits a high-priority vision job.
  - The maître d' (**Kueue**) politely walks up to Team NLP: *"Thank you for using the borrowed GPUs. Team Vision is back (**`reclaimWithinCohort`**)."*
  - Kueue checkpoints Team NLP's lowest-priority batch job, pauses it, and hands the 8 GPUs back to Team Vision in **under 10 seconds**!

## 1. Team NLP `ClusterQueue` with Borrowing Limits

```yaml
apiVersion: kueue.x-k8s.io/v1beta1
kind: ClusterQueue
metadata:
  name: nlp-cluster-queue
spec:
  cohort: enterprise-ai-rnd            # Binds to shared R&D Cohort!
  queueingStrategy: BestEffortFIFO
  # 1. Preemption & Fair-Sharing Policies
  preemption:
    reclaimWithinCohort: LowerPriority # Reclaims quota from lower priority borrowing jobs
    withinClusterQueue: LowerPriority
  resourceGroups:
    - coveredResources: ["nvidia.com/gpu"]
      flavors:
        - name: nvidia-h100-flavor
          resources:
            - name: "nvidia.com/gpu"
              nominalQuota: 8          # Guaranteed 8 GPUs
              borrowingLimit: 8        # Can burst up to 16 GPUs if Vision is idle!
```

## Preemption & Eviction Mechanics

```text
 The 3-Step Kueue Preemption Algorithm:
 1. Workload Submission: Team Vision submits a Job requesting 8 GPUs.
 2. Quota Check: Vision has used 0/8 nominal quota, but all 16 cluster GPUs are busy (NLP is using 16).
 3. Target Selection & Preemption:
    - Kueue identifies that NLP is currently borrowing 8 GPUs.
    - Kueue locates NLP's lowest-priority Workloads.
    - Kueue evicts/suspends NLP's borrowing Workload (`spec.suspend: true`).
    - Kueue admits Vision's Workload immediately!
```

## Common Interview Questions

**Q1: What is a "Cohort" in CNCF Kueue, and what problem does it solve?**

A **Cohort** is a grouping mechanism that binds multiple independent `ClusterQueues` into a shared, cooperative resource pool. In traditional static quota models, unused GPU quotas assigned to Team A sit idle even when Team B has a massive backlog of training jobs. By joining a Cohort, `ClusterQueues` can **lend their idle GPU capacity to sibling teams dynamically**, maximizing expensive GPU hardware utilization across the enterprise while strictly respecting guaranteed baseline quotas.

**Q2: What is the difference between `nominalQuota` and `borrowingLimit` in Kueue?**

- **`nominalQuota`**: The **guaranteed baseline capacity** assigned to a team's `ClusterQueue`. Workloads running within nominal quota cannot be preempted by sibling queues in the cohort.
- **`borrowingLimit`**: The **maximum extra capacity** above nominal quota that a queue is allowed to borrow from idle cohort peers (e.g. `nominalQuota: 8`, `borrowingLimit: 8` allows bursting up to 16 total GPUs).

**Q3: How does Kueue handle preemption when a lending queue requires its quota back?**

When Team A is borrowing quota from Team B, and Team B submits a new job:
1. Kueue's **`reclaimWithinCohort`** preemption engine detects that Team B is requesting resources within its own `nominalQuota`.
2. Kueue identifies the active workloads in Team A that are currently consuming borrowed capacity.
3. It selects Team A's lowest-priority borrowing workload, triggers a graceful eviction/suspension (`spec.suspend: true`), and instantly admits Team B's job.

**Q4: What is the purpose of `lendingLimit` in a `ClusterQueue`?**

`lendingLimit` defines the maximum amount of quota that a `ClusterQueue` is willing to share with other queues in the cohort. If Team A has a `nominalQuota: 16` and a `lendingLimit: 8`, it ensures that at least 8 GPUs will always remain physically reserved as a local buffer for Team A, preventing sibling teams from exhausting 100% of Team A's idle capacity.

**Q5: How does Kueue prevent "Preemption Thrashing" (ping-pong evictions between teams)?**

To prevent preemption thrashing (where two teams continuously evict each other's jobs in an infinite loop):
1. Kueue enforces strict **Nominal Quota Immunity**: A workload running strictly within its own queue's `nominalQuota` is mathematically **immune from cohort preemption**.
2. Only workloads that are actively consuming **borrowed capacity** can be targeted for reclamation preemption.
3. Workloads can specify a `borrowingPreemptionCooldown` to prevent rapid re-borrowing cycles.
